    The 50/30/20 Rule for Beginners

    Digital Safety

    UPI Golden Rule: PIN Means Debit

    Entering your UPI PIN is STRICTLY for paying money out of your account. You NEVER need to enter a PIN or scan a QR code to receive cashback or payments.
